Animated architecture (text version)

The shop in its landing zone

  1. Shoppers reach Application Gateway + WAF in the shop's spoke. It forwards privately to zone-redundant App Service.
  2. App Service uses Key Vault, SQL Database and Storage over private endpoints.
  3. Calls to services outside Azure leave through the hub's Azure Firewall, over the hub-spoke peering.
  4. Application Insights and diagnostic settings send telemetry to the central Log Analytics workspace.
  5. Azure Policy, assigned at the management groups, keeps the spoke inside the guardrails.
  6. The platform team owns the hub and shared services. The shop team owns everything in its spoke.

Checkpoint: In an Azure landing zone, who typically owns the hub's Azure Firewall, and who owns the shop's Application Gateway?
  • The platform team owns the hub. The workload team owns the Application Gateway in its spoke
  • The workload team owns both
  • Microsoft owns the hub; the platform team owns the gateway

Answer: The platform team owns the hub. The workload team owns the Application Gateway in its spoke. Learn's landing zone baselines separate platform and workload ownership: the platform team runs the hub, and Application Gateway and its public IP live in the workload's spoke.
