Animated architecture (text version)

From motivation to strategy

  1. Motivations first. Learn groups them: reduce business risk, accelerate innovation, enhance agility and efficiency.
  2. A mission gives direction and purpose. Objectives and KPIs make progress measurable.
  3. Define a strategy team. Core members typically come from IT, finance and security.
  4. Prepare the organization: leadership buy-in, aligned strategies, operating-model readiness, partners.
  5. Inform the strategy through four lenses: cost efficiency, resiliency, security, sustainability.
  6. Then revisit it. Learn: "Strategy isn't a one-time exercise."
