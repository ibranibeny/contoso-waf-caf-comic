CAF Govern in five steps

  1. 1
    Build a cloud governance teamSet it up before workloads are deployed.
  2. 2
    Assess cloud risksPriority = probability × impact. Categories include compliance, security, operations, cost, data, resources and AI.
  3. 3
    Document policiesEvery risk gets one or more governance policies.
  4. 4
    Enforce policiesAzure Policy first. Supplement it with Microsoft Defender for Cloud (security), Microsoft Purview (data) and more.
  5. 5
    Monitor complianceFor example with the Azure governance workbook. Governance is never "finished".
After step 1, steps 2–5 repeat in a cycle.

Govern · enforce

Azure Policy in one minute

  • Learn: Azure Policy "helps to enforce organizational standards and to assess compliance at-scale".
  • Rules are policy definitions (JSON). Group several into an initiative (a policy set).
  • Assign them to a scope, such as a management group. Everything below inherits them.
EffectWhat it does
denyBlocks a create or update that breaks the rule
auditAllows it, but marks it noncompliant
modifyAdds, changes or removes properties, such as tags
deployIfNotExistsDeploys a missing related resource, such as a diagnostic setting
  • Remediation tasks fix existing resources that fail deployIfNotExists or modify policies.
  • Learn's tip: start with audit or auditIfNotExists before you enforce.

Animated architecture (text version)

Azure Policy guardrails in action

  1. Contoso assigns an initiative at its management group. Every subscription below inherits it.
  2. deny: a deployment to a region outside "Allowed locations". Azure Policy blocks the request.
  3. modify: a resource arrives without its cost-center tag. "Add a tag to resources" adds it during the request.
  4. deployIfNotExists: after a VM is created, Policy deploys the missing diagnostic setting, so logs reach Log Analytics.
  5. audit: the resource is allowed, but it's marked noncompliant on the compliance dashboard.
  6. Remediation tasks bring existing resources into compliance for deployIfNotExists and modify policies.

✎ "Allowed locations" (Deny) and "Add a tag to resources" (Modify) are built-in examples Learn names.
