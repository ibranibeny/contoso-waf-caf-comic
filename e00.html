The big idea

Two frameworks, two different jobs

🧭 Cloud Adoption Framework (CAF)

  • For: decision makers responsible for Azure adoption.
  • Scope: the organization's whole journey, in seven phases: Strategy, Plan, Ready, Adopt, Govern, Secure, Manage.
  • Outcome: Learn says "an Azure landing zone and operational standards".

🏗️ Well-Architected Framework (WAF)

  • For: the team that designs and runs a workload.
  • Scope: one workload: app resources, code, AI models, data and infrastructure that work together for a business outcome.
  • Five pillars: Reliability, Security, Cost Optimization, Operational Excellence, Performance Efficiency.
Where they meet: CAF builds the landing zone and the rules. Workload teams deploy into a workload landing zone and use WAF to design what goes in it.

BayuThe Cloud Adoption Framework, CAF, is the map for the whole journey: why we go, how we plan, how we prepare Azure, then how we move, govern, secure and manage.

SoraThe Well-Architected Framework, WAF, is the shipwright's guide. It helps a workload team design one workload well, across five pillars.

DewiSo CAF is for the whole fleet... and WAF is for each ship?

BayuThat's our picture of it. In Azure terms, the harbor where the ships land is called an Azure landing zone.

Animated architecture (text version)

How CAF and WAF fit together

  1. Strategy: decide why Contoso adopts Azure and how success will be measured.
  2. Plan: turn the strategy into an actionable plan: operating model, skills, inventory, costs.
  3. Ready: build the Azure landing zone. The platform landing zone holds shared governance, security and services.
  4. Adopt: migrate, modernize or build each workload into a workload landing zone.
  5. The platform's guardrails (policies, network, identity) apply to every workload landing zone.
  6. Inside its landing zone, each workload team designs its workload with WAF's five pillars.
  7. Govern, Secure and Manage never stop: they run across the whole estate.

✎ Our simplified picture. CAF guides the organization; WAF guides each workload inside its landing zone.

Checkpoint: The shop team asks: "How do we keep our web app running if one datacenter in the Azure region fails?" Which framework answers that first?
  • The Cloud Adoption Framework, because it covers the whole company
  • The Well-Architected Framework, because it guides the design of one workload
  • Neither. That's only an Azure Policy setting

Answer: The Well-Architected Framework, because it guides the design of one workload. Reliability is one of WAF's five pillars, and WAF guides the design of a single workload. CAF guides the organization-wide adoption journey.
